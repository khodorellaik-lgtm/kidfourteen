Bio

“Kid Fourteen has long been one of the gems of Beirut’s underground scene, carrying the torch for an otherwidse negelected corner of Arab indie pop, laden with dreamy synths and foggy, deep vocals that often translate onstage into dark bursts of post-punk performance energy.”

Kid Fourteen is the moniker of Lebanese musician Khodor Ellaik. Emerging from Beirut’s Punk and No Wave scenes in 2015, his work has moved over the past decade through songwriting, electronic music, experimental saxophone and ambient recordings. His live performances have taken him through underground scenes across the Levant and Europe, including Belgium, Germany, Egypt, Turkey, Armenia, Switzerland, France and Poland. He has also performed on institutional stages such as the Moscow Museum of Modern Art, S.M.A.K. in Ghent, the Palais des Beaux-Arts, the Swedish Institute and the National Theatre of Catalonia.

Kid Fourteen's debut album Dream Kids Never Sleep was an experimental pop record released in 2016 on the Lebanese label Ruptured. Two years later, after inviting Xiu Xiu to play in Beirut, Jamie Stewart introduced Khodor to the UK label Bad Paintings. The label released his second album Blood-thick Silence in 2018, a Noise Rock record driven by drum machines, sparse synths and guitars. Following several years of extensive touring, he recorded, arranged, mixed and mastered Love, a synth/electro/pop album released on his own label Kidsplay in 2020. After moving to Paris in 2022, he released his fourth album A Brief Emotional Dragon in 2023, drawing inspiration from Westerns and Japanese synth music. His instrumental album Heart is a Malady, released in 2024, is a sonic exploration of Arabic poetry. His sixth album Far Away and Well Adjusted, released in October 2026, finds him returning to songs after several years spent pushing his music towards longer compositions and instrumental work, carrying that journey back into something more intimate and unguarded.
